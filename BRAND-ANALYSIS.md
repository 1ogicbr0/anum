## 6. Things to flag before building

1. **No owned channel exists.** The site will be the first; it should solve the obvious gap
   (prices, catalogue, how to order) rather than just mirror Instagram.
2. **Captions are partly templated/AI-written.** Two captions contain leaked prompt text
   ("💜 Instagram Caption", "Make a video of this image / Setting the scene"), one uses another
   brand's hashtag (#ManhaJewellery), and identical copy appears on unrelated small brands.
   Reuse the *lines* in section 2, but write original site copy.
3. **Imagery is mixed AI + real.** For an ecommerce catalogue we will need real product shots
   from the client (or explicit sign-off to reuse the AI renders).
4. **Ordering is DM-based.** A WhatsApp/Instagram "order" CTA is the realistic phase-1 bridge;
   cart + checkout can be phase 2.
5. **Data needed from client:** price list + SKUs, product photos, logo/brand files (confirm
   lilac/purple hex), WhatsApp number, delivery charges/timelines, payment methods (COD?),
   ring sizes, return policy, "About Anum" story, customer reviews (highlight "Muse Reviews").

## 7. Recommendations for the React site

**Site type (phase 1):** lookbook + catalogue with "Order on WhatsApp / Instagram" per product.
**Phase 2:** cart, checkout, COD.

**Information architecture**
- Home: hero (lilac satin, gold piece), "Jewellery for the woman who inspires", featured
  collections, Gold/Silver toggle teaser, gifting strip, Instagram feed, reviews.
- Shop by category: Rings · Earrings · Sets · Bracelets · Handchains · Pendants · Anklets
  (mirrors highlights exactly).
- Collections: Lilac Éclat · White Glow · Starlit Grace · Élan · Amour Éclat.
- Customise: signet-ring page with live initials-engraving preview.
- Gifting: gift box, MUSE Charm add-on, "for your best friend / sister / partner" edits.
- Ring size guide.
- Reviews (from "Muse Reviews" highlight).
- About (Anum's story) · Contact / How to order · FAQ (delivery, anti-tarnish care).

**Design tokens**
- Backgrounds: lilac #D8CBEF / #C9B8E8, white; text: deep purple #4B3A7A–#7B5FC2 + charcoal.
- Accent: gold gradient (#C9A24A → #E6C87A); silver variant for the silver line.
- Type: italic serif/script display (e.g. Cormorant Garamond Italic / Playfair Display Italic)
  + clean geometric sans body (e.g. Inter / Manrope); logo-style lowercase bold rounded sans.
- Motifs: ✨ sparkle, ♡ heart, satin texture, soft lilac florals, rounded corners, generous
  whitespace, pill buttons.

**Feature ideas drawn directly from their content**
- "Which one is your Muse?" Gold/Silver toggle on product cards.
- Initials engraving preview for the signet ring.
- "Pick your mood" 1–6 ring selector for Lilac Éclat.
- Nationwide-delivery + anti-tarnish + lightweight trust strip (their own 4 icons).
- Instagram embed / reel wall and influencer collab section.
- Discount-code field (they already run influencer codes).
